# Environment lighting

`ProcessEnvironmentImage` is the deterministic CPU preprocessing step for managed Radiance HDR environments. `EnvironmentLightingCache` imports and processes an HDR asset on first use, uploads the outputs as RGBA32F textures, and caches them by stable asset UUID for the lifetime of the renderer/project cache. It produces an environment cubemap, cosine-weighted diffuse irradiance cubemap, GGX importance-sampled specular cubemap mip chain, and split-sum BRDF integration LUT. No preprocessing or upload occurs per frame. General hot reload is not implemented.

The validation scene's Studio Small 01 source, official CC0 license, retrieval date, and checksums are recorded in [Poly Haven HDRI provenance](PolyHavenHDRI.md).

The equirectangular mapping is `u = atan2(z, x) / (2π) + 0.5` with horizontal wrap, and `v = acos(y) / π` with vertical clamp. Cubemap faces use the fixed `+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z` order, with Vulkan/NVRHI-compatible face directions. Mip zero of the prefiltered specular cube has roughness zero; the final mip has roughness one.

Default output sizes are 256 environment, 32 irradiance, 128 specular, and 256 BRDF LUT, with 128 deterministic Hammersley samples per convolution/integration operation. The processor bounds output storage to 64 MiB, each cube face to 1024, the LUT to 512, and sampling to 1024 per output texel. Diffuse irradiance stores the cosine-weighted integral and the shader divides by π. Prefilter roughness maps linearly from zero at mip zero to one at the last mip. The BRDF LUT stores the split-sum scale and bias coefficients.

`EnvironmentLightComponent` stores one HDR asset UUID and a non-negative unitless radiance multiplier. Its environment orientation is the owning entity's hierarchy-composed rotation (`parentWorld * local`); translation and scale do not affect the environment. Sampling rotates world directions by the inverse environment rotation. The scene currently permits at most one environment component. Environment IBL adds to the authored directional light; an environment without a directional light remains lit, while no environment and no directional light remains black.

Radiance `.hdr` import is linear floating-point RGBA32F and does not clamp values or apply gamma conversion. Base color remains sRGB. The shader uses diffuse irradiance and split-sum GGX specular IBL with roughness-selected prefilter mip levels, plus a fullscreen environment background that follows camera rotation and ignores camera translation. RGBA16F Vulkan offscreen color targets are now available as a resource foundation, but the visible scene target is still LDR and HDR radiance above its range clips. Exposure and tonemapping are not implemented. Physical-device support for the requested RGBA16F attachment/sampling features is checked at resource creation and must be verified on the target GPU.
