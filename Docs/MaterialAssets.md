# Material Assets

PulseForge `.material` assets provide glTF-style metallic/roughness shading and OPAQUE, MASK, or BLEND visibility. Every managed material and referenced image is identified by its stable asset UUID and must have an adjacent `.meta` sidecar. The renderer resolves optional maps through the active project registry and retains material bindings until the asset is saved and the renderer refreshes its cache.

Version 1 contains a base-color texture and factor. Version 2 adds scalar metallic and roughness factors. Version 3 adds optional metallic-roughness, tangent-space normal, occlusion, and emissive maps, plus their factors. Version 4 adds `alphaMode`, `alphaCutoff`, and `doubleSided`. Versions 1–3 remain readable and default to OPAQUE, a cutoff of 0.5, and one-sided rendering. Missing maps use neutral renderer-owned fallbacks, and saving writes v4. A material may omit the base-color map. Malformed or nonfinite values are rejected before a material is applied.

The base-color factor is linear RGBA in `[0,1]`, multiplied by the sRGB-decoded base-color map and vertex color, including vertex alpha. For MASK and BLEND, effective alpha is the product of those three alpha terms. OPAQUE ignores alpha for visibility. MASK discards fragments with alpha below `alphaCutoff` (finite `[0,1]`, default 0.5); surviving fragments are fully opaque and write depth. BLEND uses straight-alpha source-over compositing in the linear RGBA16F scene target; its source RGB is not premultiplied. The packed metallic-roughness map is linear data: green multiplies roughness and blue multiplies metallic. Normal maps are linear tangent-space vectors; `normalScale` scales the tangent-space X/Y components. The occlusion map's red channel and `occlusionStrength` affect indirect diffuse response only. Material AO combines conservatively with SSAO using the lower visibility value; neither attenuates direct lights. The emissive map is sRGB-decoded and multiplied by a finite nonnegative linear `emissiveFactor` (each component up to 10,000), then added to scene-linear HDR radiance. It does not provide emissive GI.

Base-color and emissive images use sRGB GPU formats. Metallic-roughness, normal, and occlusion images use linear UNORM. Imported images receive a complete CPU-generated mip chain; color mips are averaged in linear light before being encoded back to sRGB. Texture cache keys include the requested GPU format so a source image can be used with the correct interpretation in separate roles. The shared material sampler uses linear filtering and repeat addressing.

`doubleSided` disables back-face culling for that material and flips the interpolated geometric normal on back-facing fragments before normal mapping. It is also honored by the SSAO prepass and shadow passes. MASK materials cast cutout silhouettes in directional, spotlight, and point-light shadows. BLEND materials are excluded from opaque shadow maps and the SSAO normal/depth prepass.

Transparent objects are rendered after opaque and masked geometry, depth-test against that geometry, and do not write depth. They are sorted back-to-front by transformed entity origin, with entity UUID as deterministic tie-breaker. This object-level ordering is not order-independent: intersecting meshes and complex transparent geometry may need to be split by an artist. PulseForge does not yet implement physical transmission, refraction, colored translucent shadows, or emissive global illumination.

The Editor Content Browser's material details view provides editable factors, alpha mode/cutoff, double-sided state, and UUID-backed selectors for all maps. Changes stay in a draft until **Save Material** is pressed; **Revert Material** reloads the saved asset. A successful save keeps its asset UUID and refreshes the active SceneRenderer's material resources, including the render classification and compatible pipeline.

The static glTF importer reads geometry, vertex colors (including `COLOR_0` alpha), and tangent attributes; glTF material/image/texture relationships are not imported automatically.
